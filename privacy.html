Visit Count: privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Visit Count ("the app") is made by KopiBoss ("we", "us"). You can contact us at hello@holypanda.com.

The short version

  • Everything you enter stays on your phone or tablet. We never receive it.
  • There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The app asks for no permissions (no camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or notifications) and does not use the internet.
  • You decide if and where any backup or spreadsheet leaves your device.

What the app stores, and where

The app keeps the following on your device only, in a database file inside the app:

  • the outlets you add (name, optional address, opening hours);
  • the stalls in each outlet (name, stall type, average order value you type in, opening hours and closed days, and an optional tenant name);
  • your visits (start and end time) and one timestamp for every order you tap, including undo actions;
  • your settings (the width of the estimate range, vibration, keep-screen-awake) and the time of your last backup.

The app does not record customers or other people: no photos, names, phone numbers or payment details.

About the tenant name. This field is optional free text. Please use the tenant's trading name, and do not enter phone numbers, ID numbers, home addresses or other sensitive details.

Who can see it

Only people who can unlock and use your device. We cannot see your data, because the app never sends it anywhere. The app has no password of its own, so please protect your phone or tablet with a passcode.

Backups and spreadsheets

  • If you choose Back up now or Export, the app creates a file and opens your device's share sheet. You choose where it goes (for example email, a cloud drive or AirDrop). Once you send a file to another service, that service's own privacy policy applies.
  • Your device's own backup (such as iCloud on iPhone and iPad, or Google's automatic backup on Android) may include the app's data, depending on your device settings. Apple and Google control those backups, not us.

No sharing, no selling, no tracking

We do not receive your data, so we cannot share or sell it. The app contains no advertising or analytics code, and no code that connects to the internet. The open-source software libraries it is built with run only on your device.

Children

The app is made for adults (age 18 or over) who manage a business or premises, such as a food outlet. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly handle information from children.

Keeping and deleting your data

You are in control:

  • delete a visit inside the app: it disappears from every screen and is left out of every estimate;
  • archive a stall or an outlet inside the app, which hides it from the lists while keeping its history;
  • delete the app, which removes all of its data from your device, including anything hidden or archived;
  • delete any backup or spreadsheet you created, in the place you saved or sent it, and any device backup in your Apple or Google account settings.

Because we hold no copy of your data, we cannot recover it or delete it for you. Please keep your own backups.

Your rights

Because we never receive or hold your data, there is nothing for us to give you access to or correct: it is on your device, under your control. If you have a question about privacy, write to us at hello@holypanda.com.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your data (for example, if we add online backup in the future), we will update this page, change the date at the top, and tell you in the app or in its store description before the change takes effect.
