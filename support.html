Visit Count: support

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Need help with Visit Count? Write to hello@holypanda.com. Please tell us what you did, what you expected and what happened (a screenshot helps), and which device and version of iOS you use. We read every message and reply as soon as we can.

Common questions

Where is my data?
On your phone or tablet only. We never receive it. See the privacy policy.

How do I back up my data?
Open Setup, then Export and backup, then Back up now. Keep the file somewhere safe, such as your email or a cloud drive. Back up before changing or resetting your phone.

How do I move my data to a new phone?
Back up on the old phone, send the file to the new phone, and on the new phone use Restore from backup. Restore replaces everything on that phone with the backup.

How do I get my counts into a spreadsheet?
Setup, then Export and backup, then Export spreadsheets (CSV). You get files you can open in Excel or Numbers.

How do I get a refund?
Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are too. Ask Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot refund a purchase ourselves, but write to us if something is wrong with the app.

I counted an order by mistake.
Tap Undo at the bottom of the Count screen. Every undo is kept in the order log.

Why are the numbers ranges and not exact amounts?
Estimates come from the orders you saw during the hours you watched, so they are ranges. They do not include orders you could not see, such as delivery and phone orders, so they may run low. See the terms of use.

How do I delete my data?
Delete a visit in History (open the visit, then the menu), or delete the app to remove everything from your device. Backups and spreadsheets you made, and any backup of your device, are separate: delete them where you saved them. See the privacy policy.

The app is hard to read or use with large text or a screen reader.
Please tell us what you see. We want it to work for everyone.
