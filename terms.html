What the app is

Visit Count helps you count the orders you see at the stalls of an outlet and turns those counts into rough sales ranges. It is a tool for gathering your own evidence.

Estimates are not exact takings

  • Every figure is an estimate shown as a range, never an exact amount.
  • The app counts only orders you could see. Delivery, phone and other orders you did not see are not included, so estimates may run low.
  • Estimates depend on the average order values you enter, the hours you watched and the days you chose to watch. A Partial estimate says the data covers only part of a stall's opening hours.
  • The app is not accounting, audit, legal or tax advice, and an estimate is not proof of any tenant's or anyone's actual takings.
